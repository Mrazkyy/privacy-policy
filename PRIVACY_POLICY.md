# Privacy Policy for Word Runner

*Last updated: [add date]*

Word Runner ("the App") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any
personal data.

## What the App stores

The App saves a small amount of data **only on your own device**, using
standard on-device storage:

- Your best score, so it can be shown to you next time you play.
- Your light/dark theme preference.

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us, to Apple, or to
any third party. The App does not use the internet at all — it works fully
offline.

## No third-party services

The App does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or any third-party
SDKs of any kind.

## No account or personal information

The App does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for or
collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other
personal information.

## Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children, because it does not collect any information at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this changes in the future (for example, if a leaderboard or other online
feature is added), this policy will be updated accordingly, and the App Store
listing will reflect that update.
